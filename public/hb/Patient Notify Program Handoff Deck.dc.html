Program Overview

What Patient Notify does for Happen Bank

A growing number of states — including New York and California — now require patients to complete third-party financing applications on their own device, not an office computer or staff-assisted form.

Patient Notify keeps Happen Bank's dental and medical office partners compliant while making financing easy for patients to discover and apply for themselves — via QR code or text.

The Current Program

The compliant SMS / QR flow

This is the piece that keeps offices compliant in states requiring patient self-service applications.

1. Signage in office

Placards let patients scan a QR code or text a Client ID to receive the financing application link — no staff involvement needed.

2. Double opt-in SMS

Texting the shortcode triggers an opt-in message; replying YES delivers the application link by SMS.

3. Landing page

The patient lands on a Happen Bank application page routed by their office's unique Client ID.

No marketing follow-up is sent after opt-in — this is a one-time, patient-initiated conversation.

The Current Program

Client portal & direct messaging

Portal invitation & login

Each office contact is provisioned a Client Portal account, timed with their shipment. User onboarding/offboarding is managed by us as new staff join or leave.

Direct messaging tool

Beyond passive signage, office staff can send an application link directly to a named patient by email from the portal, tracked as Sent → Opened → Link Clicked, with one follow-up attempt allowed.

SMS volume & QR generation

Plan includes 500k SMS messages/year; additional 250k blocks are $3,000. QR codes are generated in bulk from the office list, with new codes created automatically as offices are added.

The Current Program

Reporting & visibility

Email path

When a patient is emailed from the client portal, status is tracked as: Email Sent → Email Opened → Application Link Clicked. One follow-up attempt is available.

SMS path

Patient-initiated SMS is tracked as: SMS Initiated → Opted In → Link Clicked. Name and email are not captured for this path.

Monthly to Happen Bank

Deliveries and adoption are shared at the end of each month, feeding into a new 2026 performance dashboard built specifically for this partnership.

Honest Assessment

The program hasn't hit its potential yet

Awareness in-office

Materials aren't always displayed consistently, and front-desk turnover means new staff often aren't aware the program exists.

Patient awareness

Patients don't always notice the signage or understand what scanning the QR code will do for them.

Reinforcement

There's little proactive follow-up with offices that go quiet — low-engagement locations aren't flagged and re-activated.

The rebrand is a natural moment to reset engagement and prove renewal value.

Draft — For Discussion

Recommendations to drive adoption

01

Re-launch with the rebrand. Pair fresh materials with a short refresher for front-desk staff at every active office.

02

A 60-second quick-start. A simple card or short video showing exactly where to place materials and how the QR/text flow works.

03

Proactive dashboard alerts. Flag offices with zero scans in 30 days and trigger a check-in before they go fully dormant.

04

Light-touch check-in calls. Tie reorders to a brief call so materials aren't just reprinted, but re-engaged with.

05

Spotlight champion offices. Share monthly performance with Happen Bank and highlight top-performing locations as a model for others.

06

Simplify the ask. Reduce friction anywhere staff still need to intervene — the goal is a fully patient-driven experience.

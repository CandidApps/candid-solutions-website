Executive Summary

On April 24, 2026, Nuvia Dental Implant Centers processed 41 refund transactions totaling $79,522.93 across 17 locations. These produced 17 negative batch closings with a combined net of −$69,521.93. Under standard processing protocol, each negative batch should generate a Settlement/Payment debit against the merchant's account to fund the cardholder credits.

By cross-referencing all three data sources — the Transaction Report (BatchId per transaction), the Batch Report (batch net per BatchId), and the Funding Report (Settlement events per MID) — it is possible to trace every Settlement/Payment entry to its originating batch. This analysis confirms that all 10 Settlement/Payment entries in the Funding Report correspond to negative batches from dates other than April 24: two to pre-4/24 batches outside the reporting window, and eight to post-4/24 batches on April 28, April 30, and May 1 that Nuvei settled on their normal 2–4 day cycle.

Not one of the 17 April 24 negative batches has received any Settlement/Payment recovery. Nuvei has continued processing subsequent batches normally while the April 24 negative batches — totaling $69,521.93 — remain entirely unsettled twelve days later.

02

Methodology: Three-Way Reconciliation

ℹ How the Reports Were Linked
The Transaction Report contains a BatchId field on every transaction. Grouping transactions by BatchId and summing amounts produces the net for each batch — which can be verified against the Batch Report (covering 4/24 only) where BatchIds and net amounts align exactly. The Funding Report does not contain a BatchId field, but each Settlement/Payment entry can be matched to a specific batch by: (1) same MID, (2) matching amount (Settlement/Payment amount = batch net), and (3) funding date falling 1–5 days after the batch date, consistent with standard settlement timing. Every Settlement/Payment entry in the Funding Report was traced using this method. All 10 matched to non-April 24 source batches.
ℹ What Is a Settlement/Payment Entry
In the Funding Report, FundingEvent = "Settlement" / FundingType = "Payment" is the entry Nuvei generates when a batch closes negative. It represents the debit applied to the merchant's bank account to fund the cardholder refund credits. Other negative entries in the Funding Report — Monthly Fee deductions, Chargeback/Payment entries, Authorization/Dispute fees — are entirely unrelated to batch settlement and were excluded from this analysis.

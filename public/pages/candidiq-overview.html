The problem

Businesses overpay and burn hours on vendors. Every month.

  • Contracts auto-renew at bad rates.
  • Spend is scattered across vendors, PDFs and inboxes.
  • Nobody wants to sit on hold with the cable company, the phone provider or the processor.
  • Office managers become accidental telecom admins.
  • A simple move, add, change or cancel turns into a week of phone tag.
  • When something breaks, the customer ends up as the middleman between their staff and three suppliers.
What they do today: a spreadsheet, some email and hope. Or "our MSP handles phones," but not merchant, not renewals and not disputes.
5 to 15%overspend on telecom, merchant and UCaaS is common when nobody is watching.
$10K to $100K+a year can get locked in by one missed renewal.
5 to 10 hrsa month lost by ops leaders on vendor admin. Often more for multi-site.

Typical ranges we see. Not a guarantee for any single business.

The product

Three layers. One portal.

Software for visibility. AI for benchmarks. People for execution. That last part is the one competitors skip.

01 · VISIBILITY

What do we have?

And what does it cost?

  • My Services. Every live service and contract with providers, terms and renewal dates.
  • Documents and history. Contracts and deliverables in one place, not a shared drive folder.
  • Savings snapshot. What we found and proposed, side by side with what you pay now.
  • Tech Spend. A category view of recurring tech through a bank connection. Rolling out account by account.
02 · INTELLIGENCE

Is this a good deal?

Frank does the research.

  • Bill analysis. Upload a statement. Candid reviews it and publishes findings or a proposal.
  • Find Solutions with Frank. Plain-language shopping for phones, internet, Microsoft 365, security and more, with ranked picks and a buyer's guide.
  • Honest pricing. Published catalog prices, and Candid usually beats them.
  • Quotes and proposals. Request, compare, accept. Contracts move straight into your workbench.

Common questions

Straight answers.

We already have an MSP.

Perfect. We work alongside them. We cover the categories they usually don't, like merchant processing, renewals and multi-vendor work, and you get one portal you own.

We can call vendors ourselves.

You can. Most teams don't. Frank gives you leverage, and Candid can make the calls so your people stay productive.

Is AI replacing my office manager?

No. Frank cuts the research. Your manager approves. We handle the hold music.

Why not Vendr or another procurement tool?

Those are enterprise SaaS procurement tools. We're cross-category for businesses of every size, at a fraction of the cost, with people who execute.

What does Candid actually do for us?

One clear outcome at a time: a renewal, a quote cycle, a dispute, a move or cancel package. Defined up front so you know what done looks like.

What about security and our data?

Documents live in secured storage. Bank connections are only turned on if and when they're enabled for your account. Supplier escalations stay on our side.

How fast do we see value?

The first win is usually a bill analysis or a quote package in days, not months. Then renewals keep paying you back.

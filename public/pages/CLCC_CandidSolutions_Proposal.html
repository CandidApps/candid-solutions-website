03Payment Processing — CandidPay vs. Elavon

Based on a detailed 4-month analysis of Crystal Lake CC's merchant statements (Dec 2025–Mar 2026), CandidPay projects annual savings of $10,100–$13,300 through a combination of pricing model reform, fee elimination, and smarter AMEX optimization — plus a full suite of member-facing payment tools your club doesn't have today.

⚑ Red Flags — Current Elavon Account
⚑
5.74% all-in effective rate — Roughly 2.5–3× what Crystal Lake CC should be paying on a competitive interchange-plus program. The benchmark for a similar merchant is ≤ 3.00%.
⚑
BASC STAND fee: $124.99/month ($1,499.88/year) — Charged every single month with no transparent value statement. This fee has no equivalent in CandidPay's standard pricing and should be immediately eliminated.
⚑
Processor markup nearly equals interchange ($1,259 vs. $1,442) — An 87% markup-to-interchange ratio is a hallmark of abusive tiered pricing. Standard interchange-plus markup runs 0.20%–0.40%; Elavon is collecting 1.38%.
⚑
Non-qualified surcharges: $39–$114/month — Tiered pricing penalties penalizing premium and rewards cards. These charges disappear entirely under interchange-plus pricing.
⚑
AMEX markup at ~2.1% above wholesale — Effective AMEX cost is ~4.2% all-in. Via CandidPay OptBlue, the markup drops to 0.5%–0.8%, saving $55–$80/month on the club's AMEX volume.
⚑
Rate spikes to 6.69% in low-volume months — Fixed fees of $149.12/month are devastating when volume dips (March 2026 hit 6.69% on $13,522). CandidPay's lean fixed-cost structure protects the club in off-season Q1.

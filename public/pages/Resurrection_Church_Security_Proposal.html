Joe's Recommendation on These Items

PoE Switch: The NETGEAR GS316P (115W, ~$85–100) is the right call. Six Ring cameras at ~15W each totals ~90W — well within the 115W budget with room to spare. The 16 ports future-proof for expansion without overspending on the 183W model.

Battery Backup: One EcoFlow RIVER 3 Plus ($269) is the smart play — its sub-10ms UPS switchover is faster than most dedicated rack UPS units, and 286Wh is enough to keep a modem + PoE switch running for 2–4 hours during a short outage. More cost-effective than two smaller units ($398 for 2× RIVER 3) and covers both devices from one station. The DELTA 3 Plus ($799+) is only worth it if extended multi-hour outages are a concern.

Mesh WiFi: Hold on this until we can get admin credentials for the current Orbi system. If it's performing adequately, we may only need the Ring Alarm Pro's built-in eero + 3 Chime Pro units for Ring device coverage. A full mesh upgrade can be revisited later if needed.

Business-Grade Alternatives to Consider

Ring is an excellent value for small-to-mid-size facilities like Resurrection. However, if the church ever requires commercial-grade features (NVR local recording, enterprise analytics, NDAA compliance), these platforms are worth evaluating:

  • Verkada — Cloud-managed, enterprise-grade cameras with AI analytics, 365-day cloud storage, zero NVR required. Ideal for multi-building campuses. Higher cost (~$300–600/camera + licensing).
  • Lorex — Professional 4K NVR systems with no monthly fees. Local storage on hard drive. More affordable than Verkada but requires more hands-on management. Great for organizations wanting to avoid cloud subscriptions.
  • Ubiquiti UniFi Protect — Self-hosted NVR system with no recurring fees. Very popular with IT-savvy organizations. Excellent camera quality at mid-range pricing. Requires UniFi hardware ecosystem.
  • Arlo Pro — Wire-free cameras with excellent video quality (2K HDR). Good middle ground between Ring's simplicity and enterprise features. Free local storage with every camera.

For Resurrection's scale and budget, Ring remains the strongest fit — combining ease of use, affordable cloud storage, comprehensive ecosystem integration, and Candid Solutions' partner pricing advantage.

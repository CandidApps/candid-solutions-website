Pitches

30 seconds
CandidIQ is the operating system for business technology spend. One portal where a company sees every contract, uploads a bill, requests a quote and opens a move, add, change or cancel. Real specialists call the suppliers while Frank, our AI, benchmarks what things should cost. One portal. Honest benchmarks. We make the calls.
One minute · investor
Candid Solutions has spent 20+ years as a broker and MSP across 300+ suppliers: phones, internet, payments, cloud and security. That supply chain and negotiation muscle is hard to copy. CandidIQ productizes it. Software gives visibility. Frank reads bills and surfaces honest pricing. Candid specialists in the Action Center RFQ suppliers, negotiate and handle support so the customer stays off hold. Enterprise tools like Vendr cost $20K to $100K+ a year and leave the work to the buyer. Spreadsheets and MSPs are fragmented. We serve businesses of every size, cross-category, with humans who execute. The model is subscription, plus Candid's commission economics on placed deals, plus retention because the portal becomes the system of record.
CustomerOwner or ops
CandidIQ is where you run technology spend. You see every service and renewal, drop bills for analysis, request quotes, and open move, add, change or support tickets. Candid's team does the supplier work. Frank tells you what a fair price looks like. You approve. You stop living on hold.
Investor
We're productizing a twenty-year cross-category brokerage into a portal. Software for visibility, AI for benchmarks, humans for execution. Competitors are either enterprise SaaS tools that don't call carriers, or agents without a system of record. We own both, and the economics work on subscription plus fulfillment.
Partner / MSP
Refer clients into a portal that makes you look modern while Candid fulfills. Your clients get one place for spend and support. You stay the relationship owner. A white-label partner edition is available too.

How to demo

15 minutes. Five stops.

Use the Wayne demo account or a live account you've been cleared to show.

  1. My Services

    Open on the inventory.

    "Here's everything live and when it renews."

  2. Upload and bill analysis

    Drop an invoice and show where it lands.

    "Drop an invoice. We turn it into a decision."

  3. Find Solutions with Frank

    Ask for Microsoft 365 or phones.

    "Ranked picks, and a Quote button right on the card."

  4. Message Center

    Open a MACD or trouble ticket.

    "No calling anyone. It goes straight to a specialist."

  5. Published proposal

    Wayne or a cleared live account.

    "This is what 'we finished it' looks like."
Always end on: approve in the portal, we execute, you stop living in your inbox.

ROI talk track

Use their numbers whenever you can.

A · Hard dollars

Rate savings

Lead with the standing line: Candid can typically save 20 to 30% below what they'd get on their own, priced off the supplier's advertised business promo price. Then prove it with their bill.

B · Time

Hours back

Use $50 to $75 an hour for an office manager. 8 hrs a month × $60 × 12 is about $5,800 a year before any rate savings.

C · Risk

What it avoids

Missed renewals, wrong product fit, no inventory for audits or acquisitions, and lost credits from disputes with no paper trail.

Rule of thumb

At $24.99 to $39.99 a month, finding even $500 a year in savings or giving back one hour a month pays for the plan. Most multi-site accounts have far more headroom than that.

Cash back: where Candid places services, members can earn cash back on eligible products. Position it as alignment. Never quote a rate.

Guardrails

Protect trust. Don't over-promise.

  • No guaranteed savings % without their bills in hand.
  • No Tech Spend or bank linking demos or promises unless that account's build is cleared.
  • No SOC 2 or enterprise SSO claims until they're live.
  • Frank is not fully autonomous. Specialists finish the work.
  • No case-study names or logos until Bryan green-lights them. Use illustrative math.
  • Sell what's live. Paid billing, full Tech Spend and deeper member Frank are still maturing.

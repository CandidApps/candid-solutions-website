How to View & Respond to Chargebacks

A chargeback response is your opportunity to dispute a cardholder's claim by providing supporting documentation. This guide covers the full lifecycle — from receiving a notification to tracking the outcome in the Nuvei Merchant Dashboard.

1
Overview

MerLink Portals

MerLink provides two separate portals for managing disputes. Both can be used to submit responses, but they offer different levels of access.

MerLink DRS Remote

Full dispute portal. Log in to manage cases, review dispute statuses, and upload documentation from any location.

merlinkdrs.com ↗

MerLink DRS Response

Lightweight response-only tool. Use this to submit documentation for a specific case without needing full portal access.

merlinkresponse.com ↗
ℹ Responses can be submitted via merlinkresponse.com regardless of whether you have been granted access to the full MerLink portal. If you have portal access, you can also log in to merlinkdrs.com to review dispute details before responding.
2
Notifications

Chargeback Notification Emails

You will receive a chargeback notification email in two situations:

  • When a new chargeback occurs
  • When the status of an existing chargeback changes

The email includes a snapshot of the Dispute tab from the Nuvei Merchant Dashboard, including: Dispute Date, Type, Amount, ARN, Transaction Date, card type, and MID. For full details, log in to the Merchant Dashboard directly.

ℹ If you received a notification but the chargeback is not listed in your portal, it is likely a retrieval request, inquiry, or a pre-arbitration notification where a debit has not yet been initiated.
3
Step 1

Review the Chargeback in the Merchant Dashboard

Log in to the Nuvei Merchant Dashboard and navigate to the Chargebacks section. Each chargeback record includes the following fields:

Merchant ID (MID)
DBA Name
Date
Date Filed
Date Resolved
Case Number
Card Type
Amount of Dispute
ARN
Auth # (AUTH)
Reason Code
Transaction Date

Use the column settings to hide fields you don't need, so you can focus on the most relevant information.

ℹ Important: The Authorization Number (AUTH #) is the common reference for a chargeback throughout its entire lifecycle. The Case Number will change each time there is a money movement or action on the case — the AUTH # stays the same.
4
Step 2

Gather Your Documentation

You must upload supporting documents to the MerLink portal within 10 days of receiving the chargeback notification. Failure to respond within the required window results in an automatic loss.

⚠ Do not issue a refund. Once a chargeback has been initiated and funds have been debited from your account, issuing a refund is not an acceptable response — it results in your account being debited twice for the same transaction.

Before submitting, collect the following:

  • Case number — found on the chargeback notice or in your Merchant Dashboard
  • Case amount — the disputed dollar amount
  • Email address — the address you will use to submit the response
  • Supporting documentation — signed agreements, invoices, purchase orders, shipping confirmations, or other relevant evidence

We recommend preparing a brief one-paragraph written summary that clearly communicates the key facts of the transaction. You will have the opportunity to include this in the Merchant Comments field during the response process.

5
Step 3

Submit Your Response via MerLink

Go to merlinkresponse.com (or log in to merlinkdrs.com if you have portal access). Enter your Case Number, Case Amount, and Email Address, then click Upload File. Follow the steps for your case type below.

Standard Chargeback

1

Click Challenge to begin your upload. This button must be clicked before attaching any documentation.

2

Click Choose File and upload your documentation. Accepted formats: PDF, JPEG, or TIF — maximum file size 10 MB.

3

Click Submit. Files uploaded before 2:00 PM Eastern Time are typically available the next business day. A confirmation message will appear on screen when the upload is successful.

Pre-Arbitration Chargeback

1

On the pre-arbitration upload screen, check the acknowledgment box before submitting:

"I understand my pre-arbitration response may be submitted to the issuer and possibly go to arbitration. Should it be ruled in the issuing bank's favor, I will be responsible for the disputed amount and could be liable for filing fees."

2

Click Choose File and upload your documentation (PDF, JPEG, or TIF; max 10 MB).

3

Click Submit. You will see a confirmation displaying "Image uploaded" along with your case number and case amount.

⚠ No email confirmation is sent for successful document submission. Watch for the on-screen confirmation message to confirm your upload went through. The issuing bank will not send status updates directly to you during the dispute process.

Dispute Lifecycle by Card Type

Each chargeback has a lifecycle defined by its Dispute Type, which describes the chargeback's status at each stage. The Dispute Type column in the Merchant Dashboard reflects the current stage.

Visa — Authorization & Fraud-Related Disputes

Dispute TypeDescription
Allocation DisputeNew Visa chargeback in the Allocation flow.
Allocation Pre-ArbitrationVisa pre-arb sent directly to issuer without debiting the merchant (e.g., system recognized an existing credit).
Allocation ArbitrationVisa's ruling on the Allocation Pre-Arbitration case.
Allocation ReversalMerchant attempt to rebut an Allocation Dispute.
Allocation Reversal Pre-ArbitrationMerchant rebuttal documentation has been submitted.
Allocation Reversal ArbitrationVisa's final ruling on the case.

7
Outcomes

What Happens Next?

The final stage of a chargeback depends on the outcome. If the merchant prevails, the process typically ends with a Reversal and the disputed funds are returned. If the cardholder challenges the decision, the case proceeds to Arbitration — the final step.

The issuing bank does not share detailed status information during the dispute process. When a chargeback is filed, the disputed amount is debited to the merchant. If the bank rules in the merchant's favor, the funds are returned. Note that even after a merchant win, the cardholder may still pursue arbitration.

Each card brand has a different window for arbitration. If the dispute status has not changed within the applicable window and funds have not been returned, the dispute can be considered lost:

Visa30 days
Mastercard44 days
Discover30 days

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my documents were submitted successfully?

A confirmation message appears on screen when documents are successfully uploaded. There is no email confirmation sent for successful submissions.

Will the bank notify me about the chargeback status?

No. The issuing bank or credit card company does not send status updates directly to merchants during the dispute process. Monitor the Dispute Type column in the Merchant Dashboard to track progress.

Why does the case number keep changing?

The Case Number changes each time there is a money movement or action on the dispute. Use the Authorization Number (AUTH #) as your consistent reference — it remains the same throughout the chargeback lifecycle.

What if I have portal access — do I still use merlinkresponse.com?

You can use either portal. Merchants with access to MerLink DRS Remote (merlinkdrs.com) can review and respond to disputes there. Merchants without portal access can always submit responses directly at merlinkresponse.com.

Who should I contact for help with a specific chargeback?

Contact your merchant account provider's risk or chargeback support team for questions about a specific case.

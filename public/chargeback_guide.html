Overview

Initial Guidance

This is a managed service designed to ensure you are not losing money to chargebacks. The contacts and steps below are your starting point whenever a chargeback notice arrives.

Chargeback Team
1-866-637-5467

Call when you receive an initial chargeback notice. Have your email open — they will walk you through submitting dispute information for best results.

Merchant Support
1-877-772-3346

Can assist with navigating the Merchant Dashboard.

When You Receive a Chargeback Notice

  1. Call the Chargeback Team at 1-866-637-5467 with your chargeback email open. They will walk you through how to best submit the dispute information to ensure the best results.
  2. Have the following information ready — all of it is available in the Merchant Dashboard → Disputes tab:
    • MID (Merchant ID) — your unique merchant account number
    • Case Number — the unique identifier assigned to the chargeback case
    • ARN (Acquirer Reference Number) — the unique transaction ID at the acquiring level
  3. Review the dispute details online at merchant.nuvei.com/disputes.

How You Receive Chargeback Notices

Disputes with reply information are delivered through multiple channels so nothing is missed:

  • Email notices sent to primary account email address
  • Separate notices via the Merchant Dashboard — log in and navigate to the Disputes tab
  • Physical documents mailed out for your records
  • Any additional information can be accessed by calling the Chargeback Team at 1-866-637-5467
⚠ There are fees involved at every stage of a dispute. The Chargeback Team can help you evaluate whether contesting a chargeback makes sense given the amount and fees involved.

Best Practices

Preventing Chargebacks

The cardholder initiates chargebacks, so it is not possible to completely prevent them. However, there are steps you can take to reduce the risk.

  1. Close the batch every day; use auto-batch settings.
  2. Use a clear DBA that customers will recognize on their bank statement.
  3. Include your phone number on receipts so customers can call if they have questions.
  4. State your return and refund policy clearly, and ensure customers acknowledge it.
  5. Provide accurate descriptions and images of the products being sold.
  6. Do not accept expired credit cards.
  7. Do not accept a credit card to satisfy a debt caused by a dishonored cheque.
  8. Obtain authorization for the full amount of the sale.
  9. Verify the card using all available methods:
    • In the US, verify the customer's name, address, and phone number using the issuing bank's Voice Authorization Centers.
    • Use the Processing Bank's fraud services, such as AVS (Address Verification) and CVV2.
  10. Charge the customer card at the time of shipping, unless you know there will be a delivery delay.
  11. For card-absent transactions, inform the customer of the delivery method and expected delivery date.
  12. Get signed proof of delivery.
  13. Work with the customer to establish a solution before the chargeback occurs.
  14. Respond to a chargeback notice within 10 days, or else you forfeit your right to dispute it.

Response Process

Respond to Chargebacks

How to respond to the first chargeback notice

  1. Do not issue a refund for the transaction. The customer has already been refunded.
    • If you have already issued a refund, void the refund (if the batch is still open). If the batch is closed, include the refund receipt in the chargeback response.
  2. Following the instructions and timelines on the chargeback notification carefully, respond within 10 days; otherwise, you forfeit the right.
  3. Note to expect a non-refundable chargeback fee on the processing statement. (American Express handles its own chargebacks.)

Response Document Guidelines — First Notice

  • Present rebuttal documents for each chargeback separately, rather than combining multiple responses.
  • Do not send photos of your documents. Documents must be as legible as if faxed or photocopied. Images will be converted to black-and-white 8.5×11-inch at 200×200 DPI.
  • Do not submit: video files; links to external sources (copy and paste the material); documentation that does not address the cause of the chargeback.
  • Rebuttals should be no more than 20 pages (per Visa and Mastercard rules) and consolidated into a single PDF file.
  • All documents must be in English or include English translations (per Visa and Mastercard rules).
  • Even if the cardholder informs you that they have cancelled the dispute with their issuing bank, continue to provide the rebuttal documents so that the chargeback case can be reviewed and resolved. Include documentation indicating the Cardholder cancelled the dispute and/or legible screenshots of the conversation.

How to respond to a second chargeback notice

⚠ Fees will begin to accumulate at this point. It is rarely worth the expense and effort unless the disputed amount is very large.

Response Document Guidelines — Second Notice

Submit, at a minimum, documentation that matches the Reason Code provided in the notification.

  • Credit previously issued on the same card account: Provide both sales and refund receipts.
  • Transaction was manually entered: Provide a card imprint.
  • Online or virtual terminal transactions: Proof of positive AVS and CVV2 responses.
  • Goods were shipped: Include the tracking manifest and/or signed shipping receipt.
  • Sales receipt with confirmed PIN (or signature on receipt, if PIN is not possible).
  • Sales receipt signature.
  • Invoice for goods or services provided.
  • Description of goods or services provided.
  • Prove the cardholder participated in the transaction, received the goods or services, or benefited from the transaction.
  • Proof of correspondence (voice transcripts, email, SMS) illustrating that the merchant spoke to the cardholder attempting to rectify the situation.

Fee Schedule

Sample Fee List

ℹ Fees can vary based on many factors. The following is for sample purposes only and should not be relied on as a reference for the amount that will be charged.
Fee Type Description Amount
Chargeback Fee Applied for each chargeback notification received for a specific merchant account. Covers the administrative costs of responding to the notification, including preparation and transmission of required paperwork. $10–$40
Dispute Resolution Fee Applies when a merchant has a representment (reversal) on their account. When a customer initiates a chargeback through their issuing bank, the merchant can accept it or decline it (reverse it). $25
Mastercard Arbitration Fee Charged to the party that withdraws or loses an arbitration or compliance case. $575
Mastercard Technical Violation Charged for a technical violation incurred during the dispute process. $200/violation
Pre-Arbitration Fee Applied when an issuer and/or cardholder reviews the rebuttal and continues to dispute the transaction, or when a representment is submitted in the Visa allocation flow. Covers card brand and administrative costs including preparation and transmission of required documentation. $40
Retrieval Fee Applied to each retrieval request received on a merchant account. Occurs when an issuing bank requires a copy of a transaction record. Covers the administrative costs of responding to the request. $10–$40

Ms. Smith says they never received the books (Maria left them on the bus). Ms. Smith informs her bank, which initiates a chargeback. The bank withdraws funds from the school account and issues a Chargeback Notification to the school. Chargeback Fee $25

📋  Notes & Disclaimers
🏛️ Colorado SB26-134 — Watch Closely

As of April 2026, the Colorado Senate is actively voting on SB26-134, which would prohibit payment processors from charging interchange fees on the sales tax portion of a transaction. If passed, processors would charge their rate on $104.00 (base + resort fee + surcharge) instead of the full $111.70 — removing the sales tax portion from the fee calculation entirely. This would likely eliminate the need for any rate reduction negotiation on credit cards and could affect how the debit rate wash is structured. The bill passed Senate committee on April 27, 2026 and was scheduled for a full Senate vote April 28, 2026.

⚠️ Tax Basis — Chaffee County, CO

All calculations based on a combined sales tax rate of 7.55% — comprised of Colorado state sales tax (2.9%), Chaffee County sales tax (2.75%), and Chaffee County lodging tax (1.9%). Rates apply to unincorporated Chaffee County. If your resort is located within a municipality such as Salida, Buena Vista, or Poncha Springs, additional city sales taxes may apply. Always verify your exact rate using the Colorado DOR GIS Rate Lookup Tool.
